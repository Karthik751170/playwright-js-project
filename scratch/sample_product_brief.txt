Eco-Friendly Smart Water Bottle Product Brief
Target Audience: Eco-conscious urban professionals aged 20-40 in Indian Tier-1 cities (Mumbai, Bangalore, Delhi).
Key Features: Vacuum insulated 18/8 stainless steel, built-in UV-C self-cleaning cap, rechargeable battery, biodegradable silicone sleeve.
Price Point: ₹1,499 per unit.
Primary Research Goals:
1. Evaluate consumer willingness to pay ₹1,499 for UV-C purification features.
2. Determine preferred color options (Matte Black, Forest Green, Ocean Blue, Rose Gold).
3. Prioritize key buying factors: Durability, Health Safety, Insulation duration, Aesthetics.
